AO

This tournament is the first big event of the year It takes place in Australia during the summer, so it’s usually very hot. Players start the season here and the matches are fast and exciting It’s known for a friendly atmosphere and long dramatic games


  • Location: Melbourne, Australia
  • Surface: Hard court
  • When: January
  • Special features
  • Very hot weather
  • Fast, powerful games
  • First Grand Slam of the year

Roland Garros

This is the toughest tournament physically. It’s played on red clay, which makes the ball slower and the rallies much longer. Players have to run a lot and fight for every point. It’s considered the most “strategic” Slam because clay requires patience and smart play


  • Location: Paris, France
  • Surface: Clay court (red clay)
  • When: May–June
  • Special features:
  • Clay is the slowest surface
  • Long rallies and very physical matches
  • Rafael Nadal is famous for dominating this tournament
Roland Garros

Wimbeldon

Wimbledon is the most famous and traditional tennis tournament. It’s played on grass, hich makes the game faster Players must wear all-white clothes, and everything feels very classic and old-school. It’s known as the “home of tennis


  • Location: London, England
  • Surface: Grass court
  • When: July
  • Special features:
  • The oldest and most traditional tennis tournament
  • Players must wear all white
  • Fastest surface in tennis

    US

    The US Open is energetic, loud, and full of excitement It’s held in New York, and the crowd is very involved. Many matches are played at night with a big show vibe It’s known for fast hard courts and dramatic moments.


    • Location: New York City, USA
    • Surface: Hard court
    • When: August–September
    • Special features:
    • Loud, energetic crowds
    • Night matches are very popular
    • Very fast hard courts
